1. Marketplace

CampusComponents connects buyers and sellers. The platform does not manufacture or guarantee components.

2. Component Quality

Buyer should check the component, specifications, compatibility and condition before accepting it.

3. Buyer–Seller Interaction

Quality, condition, compatibility, delivery and replacement issues must be handled between buyer and seller.

4. Payment & Commission

The buyer pays the component price plus applicable platform commission.

5. Refund

If payment succeeds but the seller does not deliver the component, the buyer may contact support for refund review.

6. Seller Responsibility

Sellers must provide accurate information about price, condition, description and availability.

7. Platform Limitation

CampusComponents provides marketplace and payment facilitation features and does not inspect every component.
