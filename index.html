Terms & Conditions

1. Marketplace

CampusComponents is a student marketplace platform connecting buyers and sellers. The platform does not manufacture or guarantee components listed by sellers.

2. Component Quality

Buyer should check the component, specifications, compatibility and condition before accepting it. CampusComponents is not responsible for defective, damaged, incompatible or non-working components after delivery.

3. Buyer–Seller Interaction

Questions about quality, condition, compatibility, delivery or replacement should be handled directly between buyer and seller.

4. Payment & Buyer Commission

The buyer pays the component price plus the applicable CampusComponents platform commission.

5. Quantity

Sellers must enter the actual quantity available. Buyers can purchase only the quantity currently available in the listing.

6. Refund

If payment was successfully completed but the seller does not deliver the purchased component, the buyer may contact CampusComponents for a refund review.

7. Seller Responsibility

Sellers must provide accurate information regarding price, condition, description and available quantity.

8. Platform Limitation

CampusComponents provides marketplace and payment facilitation features. It does not inspect, manufacture, guarantee or warrant every component sold by individual sellers.
